About Me

I graduated Senior High School at the University of Perpetual Help System Dalta-Molino Campus. I was a Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics (STEM) student and currently taking Bachelor of Science in Information Technology.

The reason why I took STEM as my strand is because growing up, I could only envision myself working as a radiologic technologist. However, during my Senior High School days I became aware of how difficult science courses are, to the point where I no longer picture myself working in the medical industry. It helped me to open my eyes to see the opportunities I still had in life.

Many people asked me, "Why did I take BS-IT if I was a STEM Student?" To answer that, Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BS-IT) was my second option. I considered it as my second option because way back in my 9th grade, we had a computer subject wherein we learned how to do code and explored how HTML works. Learning how to code was not easy. Yet, I found learning to code to be an enjoyable process. It piqued my curiosity to learn more about how the programming works. Thus, even though I am still not good at programming, I am confident that I can work with it and will definitely enjoy the process.
